# AI Governance Risk & Control Assessment
**Project Status:** In Progress
**Project Theme:** Can this AI system be trusted?

## 1. Project Overview

This portfolio project demonstrates a structured AI governance risk and control assessment for a fictional healthcare provider considering the use of an internal generative AI assistant.

The assessment identifies potential AI risks, evaluates proposed safeguards, documents evidence requirements, and recommends actions to support responsible AI deployment.

This is a fictional educational case study. It is not a real audit, legal opinion, or certification.

## 2. Objective

The objective is to determine what risks must be addressed before the AI system can be considered appropriate for its intended use.

The assessment focuses on:
- AI governance and accountability
- Privacy and sensitive data protection
- Information security and access controls
- AI accuracy and reliability
- Bias and fairness
- Human oversight
- Vendor risk
- Monitoring and incident response

## 3. Fictional AI System

**Organization:** ExampleCare Health  
**System:** Internal Healthcare AI Assistant  
**Assessment Stage:** Pre-deployment  
**Intended Users:** Authorized healthcare employees  
**Intended Uses:** Document summarization, internal question answering, and administrative drafting.

The system may encounter confidential organizational information and potentially sensitive patient information.

The AI is not intended to diagnose patients or independently make clinical decisions.

## 4. Assessment Methodology

The assessment uses the NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF) as its primary organizing framework.

The four core functions are:

- **GOVERN:** Establish accountability, policies, and oversight.
- **MAP:** Identify the system context, stakeholders, and potential harms.
- **MEASURE:** Analyze and evaluate identified risks.
- **MANAGE:** Prioritize risks, implement safeguards, and track remediation.

A project-specific likelihood and impact scoring model is used to prioritize risks. The scoring model is illustrative and is not an official NIST formula.

## 5. Key Risks to Assess

The assessment will consider:
- Unauthorized disclosure of sensitive information
- Incorrect or fabricated AI-generated content
- Unauthorized access to information or system functions
- Prompt injection and malicious input
- Biased or unfair AI outputs
- Inadequate human oversight
- Third-party and vendor risks
- Insufficient monitoring and incident response

These are potential risks to investigate, not confirmed findings about a deployed system.

## 6. Control Assessment

For each risk, the assessment will document:
- The risk description and potential impact
- Likelihood and impact ratings
- Existing controls, if known
- Proposed safeguards
- Evidence needed to evaluate control effectiveness
- Identified gaps and recommended remediation
- Risk owner and target completion date
- Residual risk after verified safeguards

Unknown control status will be recorded as unknown or not assessed. Proposed controls will not be represented as implemented or effective without supporting evidence.

## 7. Preliminary Governance Recommendation

The fictional system should not be approved for production use until critical risks have been evaluated, required safeguards have been implemented, and their effectiveness has been demonstrated.

The final recommendation will depend on documented assessment results, unresolved gaps, and residual risk.

## 8. Project Deliverables

- AI system profile and assessment scope
- AI risk register
- Risk and control matrix
- NIST AI RMF mapping
- Gap assessment and remediation plan
- Executive summary and governance recommendation
- Supporting references

## 9. Limitations

This project uses a fictional organization and hypothetical system. No real patient records, production systems, vendor evidence, or confidential organizational information are used.

Risk ratings are illustrative until supported by documented assumptions, testing, and evidence.

The assessment does not establish legal compliance, certify a system, or guarantee that the AI is safe.
