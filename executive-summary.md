# Executive Summary
## AI Governance Risk & Control Assessment

**Organization:** ExampleCare Health (Fictional)  
**AI System:** Internal Healthcare AI Assistant  
**Assessment Type:** Pre-Deployment AI Governance Assessment  
**Primary Framework:** NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)  
**Assessment Status:** In Progress

## 1. Assessment Overview

This assessment evaluates the governance, security, privacy, and operational risks associated with a proposed internal healthcare AI assistant.

The purpose is to identify potential risks, document proposed controls, review illustrative evidence references, and recommend remediation actions before deployment.

The assessment uses the NIST AI Risk Management Framework as its primary reference.

## 2. Key Risk Findings

Five primary risk areas were identified:

- **Sensitive Data Disclosure:** Potential exposure of sensitive personal and healthcare information.
- **Access Control:** Insufficient restrictions could allow unauthorized access.
- **AI Output Accuracy:** Inaccurate or misleading AI-generated responses could affect decision-making.
- **Audit Logging:** Insufficient logging could limit monitoring and investigation capabilities.
- **Third-Party Risk:** External AI vendors could introduce privacy, security, and compliance risks.

## 3. Recommended Risk Mitigation

The assessment recommends the following actions:

- Implement data minimization and appropriate access restrictions.
- Enforce role-based access control and multi-factor authentication.
- Establish human oversight and AI output testing procedures.
- Enable audit logging and periodic monitoring.
- Conduct third-party AI vendor risk assessments.
- Establish documented privacy and data retention requirements.

A remediation plan has been developed to assign responsibilities, prioritize corrective actions, and define validation methods.

## 4. Privacy Considerations

The privacy assessment addresses data collection, access, storage, retention, third-party sharing, transparency, and data accuracy.

Additional evidence and validation are required to determine whether proposed privacy safeguards are operating effectively.

## 5. Framework Alignment

The assessment maps governance activities to the four NIST AI RMF functions:

- GOVERN
- MAP
- MEASURE
- MANAGE

These mappings demonstrate how identified risks and recommended safeguards relate to recognized AI risk management practices.

## 6. Overall Assessment Conclusion

The proposed healthcare AI assistant presents governance, privacy, security, and operational risks that should be addressed before deployment.

The organization should prioritize sensitive data protection, access control, AI output reliability, ongoing monitoring, and third-party oversight.

Deployment readiness should not be established until required safeguards have been implemented, supporting evidence has been reviewed, and outstanding risks have received appropriate approval.

## 7. Assessment Limitations

This is a fictional portfolio assessment developed for educational and professional demonstration purposes.

The findings and evidence references are illustrative. No actual healthcare organization, production AI system, or live security environment was evaluated.

Documented controls and proposed remediation actions should not be interpreted as independently verified or certified safeguards.
